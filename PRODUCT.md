# Pocket Ledger

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users and purpose

People recording and reviewing their personal finances on iPhone. Daily entry, balances, spending, and recent activity should be easy to find and quick to use.

## Capabilities and constraints

Native SwiftUI, iOS 26 and later. The existing ledger supports multiple currencies, accounts, transactions, splits, loans, investments, physical assets, budgets, savings goals, schedules, receipts, search, and import/backup. Preserve financial calculations, on-device storage, privacy, entitlement rules, and unsaved-draft safeguards. Watch and widget layouts are outside this redesign.

## Brand commitments

Keep the Pocket Ledger name and copper accent. Follow native iOS interaction patterns and support System, Light, and Dark appearance.

## Product principles

- Balances and activity lead Home.
- Daily entry shows essential fields first; advanced options stay available on demand.
- Explanations appear where they help a decision, with consequential warnings preserved.
- Every amount retains its currency and privacy protection.
