name: spending-review
description: Answer questions about Pocket Ledger spending by date range, category, or account, with transaction details when useful.
---

Use the spending summary action for totals and the transaction history action for specific examples or details. Apply the date range, category, and account the person asked about. If no period is given, summarize this month; ask a follow-up when the requested period or account could materially change the answer.

Keep totals separate by currency. Don't combine amounts in different currencies or infer a transaction that isn't in the returned history. If no matching expenses or transactions are found, say so.
