---
name: transaction-capture
description: Record an expense, income, or transfer in Pocket Ledger with the right account and category, and confirm the details before saving.
---

When someone asks to record a transaction, use the action that adds an expense, income, or transfer. Use account and category names from Pocket Ledger. If the transaction type, amount, or account is unclear, ask before continuing. For a transfer, distinguish the source and destination accounts and keep their currencies separate. Leave the category blank when the person doesn't specify one and the app allows it.

The action asks the person to confirm the transaction details before saving. Wait for that confirmation, and report that the transaction was saved only when the action succeeds. If it fails or is canceled, say that it wasn't saved.
